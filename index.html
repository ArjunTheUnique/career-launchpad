[ The 5-Week Cycle ]

One action cycle. Five weeks. Zero analysis-paralysis.

C3 runs on a simple loop: Reflect → Synthesize → Investigate → Test → Evaluate. Each week you complete one stage and move a step closer to a plan you actually trust.

WK.01 · REFLECTMapping the Core
  • Run the Holland Codes assessment to find your top three traits (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional) and where they show up in real roles.
  • Break down five of your best past experiences with the AEIOU method — Activities, Environments, Interactions, Objects, Users — to surface skills you didn't know you had.
WK.02 · SYNTHESIZEPattern Mapping
  • Cross-check your Holland Codes and AEIOU results to find the patterns that keep repeating.
  • Draft three directions: the Probable Path, the Alternate Path, and the Imaginary Path.
WK.03 · INVESTIGATEReal-World Research
  • Learn how to find and approach working professionals in each of your paths.
  • Run structured "prototype conversations" that test your assumptions before you commit years to them.
WK.04 · TESTTiny Experiments
  • Design a small, real project — a mini-project, an open-source contribution, or a short internship — using the tools your target industry actually runs on.
  • Track your energy and focus while you work, so the data is about fit, not just output.
WK.05 · EVALUATEBuild the Roadmap
  • Review five weeks of evidence about your energy, your fit, and your direction.
  • Leave with a written 4–6 month roadmap — your next moves, already mapped out.
