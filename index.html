[ The 5-Week Cycle ]

One action cycle. Five weeks. Zero analysis-paralysis.

Reflect → Synthesize → Investigate → Test → Evaluate. Each week moves you closer to a plan you trust.

WK.01 · REFLECTMapping the Core
  • Holland Codes assessment — find your top 3 traits and where they show up in real roles.
  • AEIOU method — break down 5 past experiences to surface hidden skills.
WK.02 · SYNTHESIZEPattern Mapping
  • Cross-check Holland + AEIOU results for repeating patterns.
  • Draft 3 directions: Probable, Alternate, and Imaginary paths.
WK.03 · INVESTIGATEReal-World Research
  • Find and approach working professionals in each path.
  • Run "prototype conversations" that test assumptions before committing years.
WK.04 · TESTTiny Experiments
  • Design a small real project using tools your target industry runs on.
  • Track energy and focus — data about fit, not just output.
WK.05 · EVALUATEBuild the Roadmap
  • Review 5 weeks of evidence about your energy, fit, and direction.
  • Leave with a written 4–6 month roadmap you wrote yourself.
